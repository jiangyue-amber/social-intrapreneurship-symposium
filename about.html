What is Intrapreneurship?

Intrapreneurship empowers employees to innovate within their organizations. By fostering innovation, agility, and leadership, intrapreneurs drive meaningful change and bring new ideas to corporates.

Mining the Hidden Leaders of Tomorrow

Founded in 2023, the Social Intrapreneurship Symposium celebrates this spirit of internal entrepreneurship and highlights the incredible potential of individuals to reshape industries from the inside.
